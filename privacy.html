Janet Calendar privacy policy

Janet Calendar is a personal tool built and used only by Josh Brahm. It requests read-only access to Google Calendar data so a private assistant running on his own computer can answer questions about his schedule.

Calendar data is read on demand and is not stored permanently. To generate answers, calendar event text may be sent to an AI model provider. Calendar data is not sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.

No one other than the account owner uses this app. To revoke access, remove Janet Calendar at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
